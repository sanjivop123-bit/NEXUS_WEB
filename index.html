Questions

What is a credit?

One message to Nexus uses one credit. Free gives you 50 credits every month. Pro plans have unlimited chat.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Move between Free, Monthly and Yearly from the app at any time.

Will Nexus post without asking?

No. Nexus asks before any action that can’t be undone, and every action is written to the activity log.

Not yet. Checkout and real sign-in with each app open at launch. Until then plans and connectors work as a preview saved on your device.
